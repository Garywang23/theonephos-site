Phosphoric Acid 85% — Food Grade

Our Phosphoric Acid 85% is available in food grade for beverage and food applications and in technical grade for water treatment, cleaning and industrial processing. Supply is sourced from large, established Chinese producers with full export documentation.

Applications

  • Beverages & Food: Cola and soft-drink acidulation, flavor adjustment and food processing (food grade).
  • Water Treatment: pH control, scale prevention and cleaning formulations (technical grade).
  • Industrial Processing: Metal surface treatment, rust removal and chemical production.

Packaging and Storage

Packing: 25 kg bag, 50 kg bag, or 1000 kg jumbo bag (double-layer plastic inner bags with plastic woven outer bags).

Storage: Keep in a cool, dry, and ventilated warehouse. Avoid moisture and heat exposure to maintain quality.
