Sodium Hexametaphosphate (SHMP) - Industrial Grade

Our Industrial Grade Sodium Hexametaphosphate (SHMP), also known as Graham's Salt, is a high-performance inorganic phosphate for industrial water treatment and process chemistry. Produced according to HG/T 2519-2017, this grade supports stable performance in scale inhibition and dispersion systems.

Product Technical Data (TDS)

Item Specification (HG/T 2519-2017)
Total Phosphates (as P2O5)≥ 68.0%
Inactive Phosphates (as P2O5)≤ 7.5%
Water Insoluble Matter≤ 0.04%
Iron (Fe)≤ 0.03%
pH Value (1% solution)5.8 - 7.3

Industrial Applications

  • Water Treatment: Functions as an efficient boiler feed water softener and corrosion inhibitor.
  • Paper Making: Used as a pulp dispersant to improve paper uniformity.
  • Textile and Dyeing: Acts as a softening agent and dispersant for dyeing processes.
  • Oil and Mining: Applied as a mud dispersant and flotation aid.

Packaging and Storage

Packing: 25 kg bag, 50 kg bag, or 1000 kg jumbo bag (double-layer plastic inner bags with plastic woven outer bags).

Storage: Keep in a cool, dry, and ventilated warehouse. Avoid moisture and heat exposure to maintain quality.
