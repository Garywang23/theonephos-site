Mono-Dicalcium Phosphate (MDCP) - Feed Grade

Our Feed Grade Mono-Dicalcium Phosphate (MDCP) combines monocalcium and dicalcium phosphate for balanced phosphorus availability and cost. It is widely used in compound feeds for livestock, poultry and aquaculture.

Product Technical Data (TDS)

Item Specification (Feed Grade)
Chemical CompositionCaHPO4 + Ca(H2PO4)2
Phosphorus (P)≥ 21.0%
Calcium (Ca)≥ 14.0%
Fluorine (F)≤ 0.18%
Arsenic (As)≤ 0.003%
Lead (Pb)≤ 0.003%

Applications

  • Compound Feed: A cost-effective phosphorus source for commercial feed mills.
  • Poultry & Livestock: Balanced solubility for steady phosphorus supply.
  • Aquaculture: Used in fish and shrimp feed formulations.

Packaging and Storage

Packing: 25 kg bag, 50 kg bag, or 1000 kg jumbo bag (double-layer plastic inner bags with plastic woven outer bags).

Storage: Keep in a cool, dry, and ventilated warehouse. Avoid moisture and heat exposure to maintain quality.
