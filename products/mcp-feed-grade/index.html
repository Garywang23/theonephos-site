Monocalcium Phosphate (MCP) - Feed Grade

Our Feed Grade Monocalcium Phosphate (MCP) is produced in compliance with the GB/T 22548-2017 feed additive standard. With high water-soluble phosphorus, it offers excellent bioavailability for all animal species.

Product Technical Data (TDS)

Item Specification (GB/T 22548-2017)
CAS Number7758-23-8
Chemical FormulaCa(H2PO4)2·H2O
Phosphorus (P)≥ 22.0%
Calcium (Ca)≥ 13.0%
Water-Soluble Phosphorus≥ 80% of total P
Fluorine (F)≤ 0.18%

Applications

  • Poultry & Swine Feed: High-availability phosphorus for fast-growing animals.
  • Aquaculture: Preferred phosphorus source for fish and shrimp due to water solubility.
  • Ruminant Feed: Supplements phosphorus in forage-based diets.

Packaging and Storage

Packing: 25 kg bag, 50 kg bag, or 1000 kg jumbo bag (double-layer plastic inner bags with plastic woven outer bags).

Storage: Keep in a cool, dry, and ventilated warehouse. Avoid moisture and heat exposure to maintain quality.
