Frequently Asked Questions

What phosphate chemicals does Theone Chemical supply?

We supply technical grade, food grade and feed grade phosphates — including STPP, SHMP, TSPP, TSP, DSP, SAPP, phosphoric acid 85%, and feed phosphates DCP, MCP, MDCP and TCP.

Which product is better for detergents, STPP or SHMP?

STPP is the standard builder for detergent formulations; SHMP is more often used for water softening and dispersion. The right choice depends on your formulation and water conditions — see our comparison article above.

What is food grade phosphoric acid used for?

Mainly for beverage acidulation (such as cola drinks), flavor adjustment and food processing applications that require food-grade acidity control.

What should buyers prepare before requesting a quotation?

Product name, grade or target specification, quantity, destination port and packing requirements. With these ready, we can confirm the supply mode and quote accurately.
